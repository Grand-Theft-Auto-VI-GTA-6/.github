# Grand Theft Auto VI 🎮 [Rockstar Games]

**Grand Theft Auto VI** is an Action, Adventure, Racing video game. Developed and published by **Rockstar North**. It was released on **26 May, 2026**, for Windows.

---

## **What is Grand Theft Auto VI?** ❓  
*Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA 6)* is the next major installment in Rockstar Games' legendary open-world action-adventure series. Set in a sprawling, hyper-detailed world, players dive into a gripping modern crime saga filled with high-stakes missions, explosive chases, and unparalleled freedom.  

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/761d3c92-6322-4711-a5d2-99779ea28fc9)

Featuring a brand-new location, fresh characters, and groundbreaking gameplay mechanics, *GTA VI* aims to redefine the open-world experience.  

---

## **Key Features** 🔥  

### 🌍 **Huge Open World**  
Explore a breathtaking, living city and its surroundings—packed with hidden secrets, dynamic events, and endless activities.  

### 🎬 **Thrilling Story**  
Immerse yourself in a cinematic criminal underworld with deep narratives, unforgettable characters, and morally challenging decisions.  

### 🖥️ **Next-Gen Graphics**  
Experience ultra-realistic visuals, lifelike animations, and dynamic weather systems that bring the world to life.  

### 🕶️ **Unmatched Freedom**  
Play your way—rob stores, hijack vehicles, take to the skies, or just wreak havoc across the city.  

### 🔫 **Improved Combat & Driving**  
Enjoy refined shooting mechanics, visceral hand-to-hand combat, and adrenaline-pumping high-speed pursuits.  

### 🌐 **Online Multiplayer**  
Team up or compete in *GTA Online*'s ever-evolving universe with new updates, missions, and chaos.  

### 🎲 **New Activities**  
From illegal street races to building a criminal empire, there’s always something exciting to do.
